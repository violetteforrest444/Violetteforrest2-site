The Reader

About Me

I'm Amy, and I read tarot the way I do everything else: honestly, thoroughly, and with a healthy sense of humor.

By day, I'm a technology program leader. I've spent my career untangling complicated systems at companies like Airbnb, finding the patterns underneath the noise, and telling people what's actually going on, even when it isn't what they hoped to hear. That same instinct shapes my readings. I won't give you vague reassurance or doom-and-gloom theatrics. I'll tell you what the cards are showing, clearly and kindly.

Tarot is at the heart of my practice, but I don't read the cards in isolation. I weave in astrology to understand the energies shaping your life right now: the transits pushing you to grow, the patterns written into your natal chart, and the timing that may be at play. Numerology adds another layer, revealing the meaning behind the numbers that keep appearing in your cards, your dates, and your life. Together, they create a richer, more complete picture than any one tool could show on its own.

I'm an Aquarius sun with a Leo moon, which means I'm equal parts analyst and believer. I keep a running log of the strange words and images that surface in my mind uninvited, just to see what patterns emerge. (So far, the universe is very into Vladivostok. I'm still waiting on the explanation.) I practice with a circle of friends who started out as a tarot class and became something closer to a soul group. When I'm not reading, you'll usually find me at the pottery wheel, learning to let things take shape without forcing them.

What I value most in a reading is truth, respect, and agency. The cards don't decide your life. You do. My job is to help you see clearly enough to choose well.

Method

The Violette Spread

A ten-card layout with foundation, crossing influence, past, present, and future held at the center, flanked by a second column tracking how you see the world and how the world sees you.

It reads less like a fortune and more like a map of forces already in motion. Card positions are always confirmed with you before interpretation begins.
