About

A Second Look at What You Already Sense

Violette is a tarot practice built on one working premise: every relationship, ending, and inconvenient plot twist was, in some sense, on the itinerary before you got here.

Readings draw on traditional tarot alongside the soul-contract tradition — the idea that souls agree to certain connections and lessons before incarnating. The cards don't predict; they locate you inside an agreement you already made and mostly forgot signing.

Every session uses the Violette Spread, a ten-position layout built specifically for this work — not a borrowed template.

Method

The Violette Spread

A ten-card layout developed specifically for this practice — foundation, crossing influence, past, present, and future held at the center, flanked by a second column tracking how you see the world and how the world sees you.

It reads less like a fortune and more like a map of forces already in motion. Card positions are always confirmed with you before interpretation begins.
